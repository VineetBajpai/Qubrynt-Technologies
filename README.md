# Qubrynt Technologies Website

Responsive static website for software, web development, AI, digital marketing and advertising clients across India.

## Features
- Responsive navigation and service sections
- Dedicated 60% off eligible new website projects offer
- Interactive INR discount estimator (illustrative only)
- FAQ accordions, office address and Google Maps search link
- SEO metadata and Organization schema with India service area
- Enquiry form that prepares an email draft after email configuration

## Office
Jankipuram, Sector-H, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh 226021, India.

## Before publishing
1. Replace `YOUR_BUSINESS_EMAIL@example.com` in `script.js` with the verified business email inbox.
2. Replace `https://qubrynt.com` in metadata/schema and `sitemap.xml` with your final domain.
3. Verify eligibility, scope and terms for the advertised discount. Domain, hosting and third-party charges may be additional.
4. Test all links and the enquiry form after deployment.

This is a static site; the form does not store submissions.
